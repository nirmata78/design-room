# Part 10 · diagram specifications

Eleven figures, three sessions (four, four, three). Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p10-queue-log
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 720, titles "Queue" and "Log".
- **Left:** a queue box with message squares m5 m6 m7; three `worker` boxes each taking one; an
  acked message drawn fading with label "deleted on ack".
- **Right:** a long strip of offsets 0 … 12 all kept; two consumer-group markers above it:
  `group: fulfilment @ 9` and `group: analytics @ 4`; a curved arrow on the analytics marker
  "rewind → replay" (**highlight**).

## p10-segments
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 700.
- A partition drawn as four segment files `00000000.log`, `05000000.log`, `10000000.log`,
  `15000000.log (active)` left to right, each ~1 GB.
- Under each, a small `.index` box "offset → byte position, every 4 KB".
- Arrow "append" into the active segment. The oldest segment crossed out grey "retention deletes
  whole file".
- A lookup arrow "read offset 5,000,040 → segment 05000000 → index → scan a few KB" (**highlight**).

## p10-isr
- **Kind:** three log strips stacked, canvas 1600 × 640, labels `Leader`, `Follower 1`,
  `Follower 2`, ending at offsets 112, 110, 108.
- A vertical dashed line at 108 across all three labelled "high watermark" (**highlight**).
- Brace around all three "in-sync replicas". Consumer arrow reading up to 108 only.

## p10-group
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- Six partition boxes P0 … P5 on the left; three consumer boxes on the right; arrows P0, P1 → C1;
  P2, P3 → C2; P4, P5 → C3.
- Second row (smaller): the same with 7 consumers, C7 grey "idle: only 6 partitions".

## p10-sink-txn
- **Kind:** Seq, canvas 1600 × 720. Participants `Log`, `Consumer`, `Ledger DB`.
- 1. Log → Consumer "batch offsets 100–109". 2. Consumer → Ledger "BEGIN". 3. "insert 10
  payments". 4. "upsert consumer_offsets: p3 = 110". 5. "COMMIT" — bracket 2–5 labelled "one
  transaction" (**highlight**). 6. note: "restart → read offset from the ledger".

## p10-reorder
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 700, titles "retry with two in flight" and "partitions 4 → 8".
- **Left:** Seq `Producer`, `Broker`: m1 sent (cross, fails), m2 sent ✓, m1 retried ✓. A log strip
  below "m2, m1" (red).
- **Right:** key `acct-7` with "hash mod 4 = 3" → P3 (old messages waiting) and "hash mod 8 = 7"
  → P7 (new messages read first, **highlight**).

## p10-poison
- **Kind:** loop diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- `Message m42 (bad payload)` → `worker` (red cross "crash") → back to queue "redeliver" →
  `worker 2` (red cross) … loop drawn three times.
- An exit arrow after "attempt 5" to a `dead-letter topic` box (green, **highlight**) with "owner ·
  alert · replay tool".

## p10-lag
- **Kind:** two gauges side by side, canvas 1600 × 600.
- Each: "lag: 2,000,000 messages". Left: "at 100,000/s → 20 s behind" (green). Right: "at 500/s →
  66 min behind" (red, **highlight**).
- Caption: "measure lag in seconds".

## p10-fanout
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 760, titles "fan-out on write" and "fan-out on read".
- **Left:** `author posts` → `fan-out worker` → many small `inbox` boxes (8 drawn, label "× followers");
  `reader` → one inbox.
- **Right:** `author posts` → one `outbox`; `reader` → many outboxes (8 drawn, label "× followed")
  → `merge`.

## p10-watermark
- **Kind:** timeline, canvas 1600 × 760. X axis "event time" 12:08 to 12:14.
- Window boxes for 12:09, 12:10, 12:11, 12:12 minutes.
- Three input lanes `partition 0`, `partition 1`, `partition 2` each with a small marker of its
  watermark: 12:13, 12:12, and partition 2 at 12:09 labelled "idle" (**highlight**, amber).
- A thick vertical line at 12:09 "operator watermark = min = 12:09 → nothing emitted".
- A late event dot arriving at 12:10 window with label "late: update or side output".

## p10-checkpoint
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 720.
- `source P0`, `source P1` → `operator (keyed state)` → `sink`. A barrier marker "‖ ckpt 42"
  travelling along the arrows.
- Below the operator, a `durable storage` box "checkpoint 42: state + offsets {P0: 1200, P1: 900}".
- A red cross on the operator "crash" and dashed arrows "restore 42" and "rewind sources to
  1200 / 900" (**highlight**).
