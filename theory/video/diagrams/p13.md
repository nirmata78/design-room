# Part 13 · diagram specifications

Seven figures, two sessions (four and three). Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p13-no-breaker
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 620.
- `Caller · 1,000 calls/s` → `Dependency (down, restarting)` with a thick red arrow.
- Under the caller, a bar of 200 thread slots all amber "waiting 200 ms each".
- Under the dependency: "1,000/s of calls that cannot succeed slow its recovery" (**highlight**).

## p13-breaker
- **Kind:** state machine, canvas 1600 × 700. Three circles `Closed`, `Open`, `Half-open`.
- Closed → Open: "failure rate > 50% over ≥ 20 calls in 10 s".
- Open → Half-open: "after 30 s cool-down".
- Half-open → Closed: "trial calls succeed" (green). Half-open → Open: "trial fails" (red).
- Inside Open (**highlight**): "fail fast · serve fallback".

## p13-shared-pool
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- `Service` box containing one pool "200 threads", all coloured amber with label "waiting on
  recommendations".
- Arrows out to `Recommendations (slow, amber)`, `Inventory (healthy)`, `Payments (healthy)`; the
  last two arrows dashed red with "no thread available" (**highlight**).

## p13-cells
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 760, titles "Bulkheads in a process" and "Cells across the fleet".
- **Left:** a service box with three pools `recs: 40`, `inventory: 80`, `payments: 80`; the recs pool
  amber "exhausted", others green.
- **Right:** `Cell router (customer → cell table)` on top; below it eight cell boxes `cell 1 … cell 8`,
  each containing tiny icons LB, app, cache, DB. Cell 3 red "bad deploy" (**highlight**), caption
  "1/8 of customers affected".

## p13-static
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 700, titles "Not statically stable" and "Statically stable".
- Both: a `Control plane` box at top with a red cross "outage", and four data-plane server boxes.
- **Left:** servers greyed "refresh failed → stop serving"; an `Autoscaler` box "cannot launch".
- **Right:** servers green "serving on last known good (disk)"; note "no changes until control plane
  returns" (**highlight**).

## p13-metastable
- **Kind:** cycle, canvas 1600 × 760. Five nodes in a loop: `latency ↑` → `timeouts` → `retries` →
  `load ↑` → back to `latency ↑`.
- An entry arrow from `trigger (spike, slow backup)` into `latency ↑`, drawn dashed with a cross
  "trigger gone".
- Two breaker icons on the loop (**highlight**): "shed load" on `load ↑ → latency ↑`, and "cut retries"
  on `timeouts → retries`.

## p13-cycle
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 600.
- `Auth service` ⇄ `Config service`: arrow "keys stored in" from auth to config, arrow "authenticates
  callers with" from config to auth, both red (**highlight**).
- Caption: "normal day: both run · cold start: each waits for the other".
