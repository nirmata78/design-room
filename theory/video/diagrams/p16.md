# Part 16 · diagram specifications

One figure, half a session; the runner pairs it with part 17's. Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p16-migration
- **Kind:** timeline with two system lanes, canvas 1600 × 760. Lanes `Old system` (top) and
  `New system` (bottom); time runs left to right over "weeks".
- Seven numbered markers along the axis: ① expand (new lane appears, empty), ② capture (arrow
  old → new "CDC / dual write", continuous), ③ backfill (thick arrow old → new "history, throttled"),
  ④ verify (a comparator box between lanes "shadow reads · diffs = 0"), ⑤ switch reads (client
  read arrows move from old to new in steps 1% → 100%), ⑥ switch writes (client write arrow moves to
  new; a reverse arrow new → old appears "reverse replication", **highlight**), ⑦ contract (old lane
  fades out after a vertical dashed line "point of no return").
- Above the axis, a bracket from ① to ⑥ "rollback = switch back, nothing lost".
