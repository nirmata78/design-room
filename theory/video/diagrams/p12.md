# Part 12 · diagram specifications

Three figures, one session. Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p12-boundary
- **Kind:** timeline with bars, canvas 1600 × 620. Axis 0:00 to 2:00 minutes, a thick tick at 1:00
  "window boundary".
- A red bar of 100 requests in 0:59–1:00 and another red bar of 100 in 1:00–1:01.
- Above, the two fixed windows drawn as brackets "window 1: 100 ✓" and "window 2: 100 ✓".
- A bracket spanning 0:59–1:01 (**highlight**): "200 requests in 2 seconds · limit 100/min".

## p12-local-limit
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 700.
- One `customer · limit 1,000/s` on the left, arrows spread over a column of gateways
  `gw 1 … gw 50` (draw 6 and an ellipsis).
- Two labelled variants on the right: "each enforces 1,000 → customer can send 50,000" (red) and
  "each enforces 20 → traffic landing on 3 gateways throttled at 60" (amber).
- Bottom (**highlight**): "the limit is global → some state must be shared".

## p12-queue
- **Kind:** queue diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- A bounded queue box "capacity 8" with request squares r3 … r10, each tagged with a small deadline.
- `r7` shown greyed with a red cross at the head of the queue: "deadline passed → dropped at
  dequeue" (**highlight**).
- An arrow from the queue's tail to the server labelled "overload: serve newest first (LIFO)".
- New arrival `r11` bouncing off "queue full → reject fast".
