# Part 7 · diagram specifications

Eleven figures, three sessions (four, four, three). Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p7-write-path
- **Kind:** vertical stack, canvas 1600 × 820. Boxes top to bottom: `Application`, `write() →
  page cache (kernel memory)`, `device write cache (volatile)`, `persistent media`.
- Arrow 1 "write() returns here" ends at the page cache (red label "not durable").
- Arrow 2 "kernel flush · maybe 30 s later" to the device cache.
- Arrow 3 "fsync / FUA · durable" to the media (**highlight**, green).
- Right side: a red lightning "power loss" pointing at the two upper boxes: "everything here is lost".

## p7-btree
- **Kind:** tree plus side boxes, canvas 1600 × 780.
- A three-level B-tree: root page, three internal pages, six leaf pages (labels `8 KB page`).
- Numbered: ① a path down to one leaf highlighted, label "find leaf · load into buffer pool";
  ② a `WAL` strip on the right with a new record "page 4711: insert k" and "fsync at commit";
  ③ the leaf drawn amber "dirty in memory"; ④ a dashed arrow from the leaf to a `disk` box
  "checkpoint writes it back later".

## p7-skiplist
- **Kind:** layered list, canvas 1600 × 620. Bottom lane: 10 25 31 40 52 61 70 84 92. Lane 2: 10
  31 52 70 92. Lane 3: 10 52 92. Lane 4: 10 92. A head node on the left.
- Search path for 70 drawn in red (**highlight**): head L4 → 10 → (92 too big) down → L3 52 → down
  → L2 70 found. Caption: "about log n steps · heights random at insert".

## p7-random-writes
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- Left: `100,000 random writes/s` arrow into a `B-tree, 10× larger than memory` box.
- From it, two red arrows to a `disk` box: "100,000 random page reads/s" and "random 16 KB page
  writes for ~100 B changes".
- Disk box drawn as a gauge at 100% (**highlight**), caption "disk saturates first".

## p7-lsm-write
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 820, four numbered steps.
- `Write` → ① `WAL (append + fsync)` and ② `Memtable (skip list, RAM)`.
- ③ `Memtable full (64 MB) → flush` arrow down to `L0: 4 overlapping SSTables`.
- ④ `compaction` arrows from L0 → `L1 (10× L0)` → `L2 (10×)` → `L3 …`, levels drawn as widening
  bars. Label beside the levels: "immutable files · never modified in place".

## p7-lsm-read
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 860.
- Top-down: `memtable` → `immutable memtable` → `L0 tables (newest first) ×4` → `L1` … `L5`, one
  table highlighted per level (the one whose key range contains the key).
- Beside each table a small funnel icon `Bloom filter: no → skip` (most grey) and on one table
  "maybe → sparse index → 1 block" with a `block cache` box and a `disk` box (**highlight** on
  the one real read).
- Caption: "stop at the first match".

## p7-bloom
- **Kind:** bit array, canvas 1600 × 620. A row of 24 bit cells, indices 0–23.
- Key `"cat"` with three arrows `h1, h2, h3` to cells 3, 11, 19 (set to 1, blue).
- Key `"dog"` → cells 5, 11, 22 (blue).
- Test key `"emu"` → 3, 11, 22: all 1 → amber label "maybe (false positive)" (**highlight**).
- Test key `"fox"` → 3, 8, 19: cell 8 is 0 → green label "definitely not".

## p7-mvcc
- **Kind:** timeline, canvas 1600 × 640. Axis "timestamp" 0 to 40.
- Row `key x`: three version boxes at t = 10 "v1", t = 20 "v2", t = 30 "v3".
- Reader at t = 25 (dashed vertical line) with an arrow to v2: "sees v2". Reader at t = 35 → v3.
- A grey bracket under v1: "collectable once no snapshot ≤ 20 remains" (**highlight**).

## p7-blob-layout
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 760.
- Left box `Metadata service (consistent, partitioned)` with a row
  `photos/a.jpg → size, checksum, chunks [c1, c2, c3]`.
- Right: six `chunk server` boxes in two rows, holding chunk squares `c1`, `c2`, `c3` (each on
  different servers, with copies or fragments).
- Upload arrows: ① client → chunk servers "write chunks", ② client → metadata "commit row (makes
  object visible)" (**highlight**). Read arrow: "lookup, then fetch chunks in parallel".

## p7-rs63
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 700.
- A stripe bar split into six blue fragments `d1 … d6` and three amber `p1 … p3`.
- Arrows down to nine rack boxes `rack 1 … rack 9`, one fragment each.
- Three racks crossed out red. Caption (**highlight**): "any 6 of 9 rebuild the stripe · 1.5×
  space · repair of one fragment reads 6".

## p7-orphans
- **Kind:** two-set diagram, canvas 1600 × 680.
- Left circle "chunks on servers", right circle "chunks referenced by metadata"; overlap "live".
- Left-only region labelled "orphans", split into two zones: "older than 3 days → delete" (red)
  and "younger → keep (upload may be in flight)" (amber, **highlight**).
- Small notes for causes: "client died before commit", "object deleted".
