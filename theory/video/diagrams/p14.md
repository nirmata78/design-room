# Part 14 · diagram specifications

Four figures, one session. Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p14-histograms
- **Kind:** three small bar charts and one large one, canvas 1600 × 760.
- Top row: `host A`, `host B`, `host C` histograms over the same buckets `<10, 10–25, 25–50, 50–100,
  100–300, 300–1000 ms` (counts as bars).
- Arrows down into one large `fleet (sum per bucket)` histogram.
- On the large one, a cumulative marker "99% of count below here → p99 in bucket 300–1000"
  (**highlight**), and a dashed edge at 300 labelled "SLO threshold".

## p14-logs
- **Kind:** pipeline, canvas 1600 × 640.
- `app` → `agent (disk buffer)` → `queue (partitioned log)` → `indexers` → `search store (hot)` →
  `object storage (cold)`.
- Above the agent, a drop-order ladder (**highlight**): "1 debug · 2 sampled info · never: errors,
  audit". A separate arrow from `app` "audit → lossless path" to its own store.
- Caption: "2,000 req/s × 20 lines × 500 B = 20 MB/s ≈ 1.7 TB/day".

## p14-columnar
- **Kind:** two panels, canvas 1600 × 720, titles "Row storage" and "Column storage".
- **Left:** rows as horizontal stripes of 100 cells; a query highlight covering whole rows "reads
  100 of 100 columns".
- **Right:** columns as vertical stripes; three of them highlighted `region`, `latency`, `time`
  (**highlight**) "reads 3 of 100". Under the region column: "dictionary: europe-west1 → 3 · run
  length: 3 × 40,000".

## p14-postings
- **Kind:** list intersection, canvas 1600 × 600.
- Row `job="api"` → [3, 7, 9, 12]. Row `status="500"` → [7, 12, 20].
- Two cursors walking both lists; matches 7 and 12 circled green (**highlight**) → result
  "series 7, 12".
- Small note: "intersect shortest list first".
