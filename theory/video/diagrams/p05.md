# Part 5 · diagram specifications

Nine figures, two sessions (five and four). Rules: `diagrams/README.md`.

## p5-async-tail
- **Kind:** block diagram with two log strips, canvas 1600 × 640.
- Top strip `Leader log`: cells 90 … 100, all blue; cells 96–100 outlined red, bracket above
  "acknowledged to clients · not yet shipped".
- Bottom strip `Follower log`: cells 90 … 95 blue, 96–100 empty (dashed).
- Arrow between strips labelled "async shipping".
- A red cross over the leader strip "leader dies". Caption under the follower (**highlight**):
  "promote follower → writes 96–100 are gone · the lag is your recovery point".

## p5-lag-curve
- **Kind:** line chart, canvas 1600 × 640. X axis "time of day" 00:00 to 24:00; Y axis "lag, seconds",
  log scale ticks 0.01, 0.1, 1, 10, 100, 300.
- Baseline around 0.005–0.02 s all day (grey line).
- Three spikes with labels: 02:00 to 4 minutes "one long batch transaction"; 12:00 to 40 s "schema
  change rewrites a table"; 18:00 to 15 s "slow disk during backup".
- Caption: "lag is a distribution with causes, not one number".

## p5-dual-write
- **Kind:** two Seq diagrams side by side, canvas 1600 × 800, titles "crash between the writes" and
  "two requests race".
- **Left:** participants `Service`, `Database`, `Log`. 1. Service → DB: "UPDATE order paid · COMMIT"
  2. DB → Service: "ok". 3. note over Service: "crash" (red). 4. dashed Service → Log: "publish
  order.paid" with a cross (**highlight**). Note: "paid, and nobody downstream knows".
- **Right:** participants `Request 1`, `Request 2`, `Database`, `Log`. 1. R1 → DB "set A". 2. R2 →
  DB "set B" (DB ends with B). 3. R2 → Log "B". 4. R1 → Log "A" (**highlight**). Note at bottom:
  "database says B · index says A · for ever".

## p5-outbox
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 720, four numbered steps.
- `Service` → `Database` with a box inside the database split into two rows: `orders` (row
  "order 7 · paid") and `outbox` (row "event e91 · key order 7 · payload"), and a bracket around both
  "① one transaction".
- `Relay` box reading from the database: two input arrows "② poll outbox" and "or tail the WAL
  (CDC)" (one solid, one dashed).
- `Relay` → `Log` "③ publish · key = order 7" → `Consumers` box with a note "dedup by event id".
- Relay back to the database "④ mark sent / advance position".

## p5-multi-leader
- **Kind:** Seq, canvas 1600 × 720. Participants `User EU`, `Leader EU`, `Leader US`, `User US`.
- 1. User EU → Leader EU: "title = A" 2. Leader EU → User EU: "ok". 3. User US → Leader US:
  "title = B" (note "80 ms later") 4. Leader US → User US: "ok". 5. Leader EU ⇢ Leader US: "A"
  (dashed), 6. Leader US ⇢ Leader EU: "B" (dashed); the two arrows cross. 7. note across both
  leaders (**highlight**): "conflict found after both users saw success".

## p5-sloppy
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 760. A ring of 6 nodes `n1` … `n6`.
- The key's home set `n1, n2, n3` shaded blue; `n3` has a red cross "down".
- `Coordinator` box with arrows to n1 (✓), n2 (✓), and an amber arrow to `n4` labelled "write + hint:
  belongs to n3" (**highlight**).
- A later dashed green arrow from n4 to n3 labelled "hinted handoff when n3 returns".
- Caption: "W = 2 met · a read of n2, n3 before handoff can miss it".

## p5-merkle
- **Kind:** two trees side by side, canvas 1600 × 820, titles `Replica A` and `Replica B`.
- Each: root, 4 children, 4 grandchildren under the second child only (keep it small); leaves
  labelled "bucket 0–3", etc. Hash labels as short hex `a1f…`.
- All hashes equal except on one path: root, child 2, grandchild 3 (red on both trees, values
  differ). Dashed arrows between the trees at each level of that path labelled "compare".
- Caption: "equal roots → identical · otherwise descend only into the child that differs".

## p5-tombstone
- **Kind:** timeline, canvas 1600 × 720. Axis days 0 to 14.
- Lanes `Replica A`, `Replica B`, `Replica C`.
- Day 0: all lanes "x = v".
- Day 1: A and B "delete x → tombstone" (grey marker). C: red bar "down" from day 1 to day 13.
- Day 11: A and B "grace period (10 days) over → tombstone purged" (marker disappears).
- Day 13: C returns, still "x = v"; repair arrows from C to A and B labelled "x = v copied back"
  (**highlight**, red). Caption: "deleted data resurrected".

## p5-seed
- **Kind:** block diagram, canvas 1600 × 640.
- `Leader` box with a log strip, cells 880 … 1000, and a marker at 900 "snapshot taken here".
- Arrow ① `snapshot @ 900` from leader to `New replica`. Arrow ② `log from 901` from the leader's
  strip at 901 to the replica. Replica shows "replay → caught up at 1000".
- Note under the leader strip (**highlight**): "log must still be retained back to 900".
