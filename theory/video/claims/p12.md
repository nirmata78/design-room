## For the author

- **12.1 token bucket**: the narration says "the idle client had banked thirty tokens" after two idle
  seconds. At r = 10 that is true only if the bucket was already full before the idle period (two idle
  seconds refill 20 from empty). Buckets start full, so this holds; if a viewer asks, that is the answer.
