## For the author

- **1.0 opening story** says "a year of it fits on one machine's disks". A year at 170 GB a day is about
  63 TB (`10000*200*86400*365/1e12` = 63.1), while §1.3 and §1.4 use 8 TB usable per node. 63 TB fits on a
  dense storage server, not on the node the part sizes with. Either say "a year fits on a handful of
  machines", or name the dense box.
- **1.13 probe "forty-three nodes, budget for twenty"**: erasure-coding *all* of it gives about 21.5 nodes, so
  the "most of the bytes" answer alone does not reach twenty; the narration's next step (shorter hot
  retention) is needed, and the narration says so. No change needed; noted so the drill's answer is not
  read as "EC alone fits".
