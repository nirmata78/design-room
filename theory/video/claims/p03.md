## For the author

- **3.9**: the slide rounds 8,000 replicas per node to "about 2,700 groups led" (8,000 ÷ 3 = 2,667); from the
  unrounded 7,812 it is 2,604 and 5,208 messages a second. Within rounding; no change needed.
- **3.10, region 1 lost**: with only region 2 (two voters) and region 3 (one voter) left, all three
  survivors are needed for a quorum of three, so every commit waits for the 70 ms link and any further
  failure stops writes. The slide's "70 ms" is right; the narration could add that the margin is now zero.
