Forty engineers, five teams, six months. How do you split the work so the teams can move independently, and what do you own as the manager? A full reference solution at the principal and senior engineering-manager bar: theory first, a three-version team-to-component map, six challenges (the critical path, a late dependency, a shared table, a contract dispute, a lost team, and how you would know the split is wrong). 51 minutes, 404 numbered slides, with an interviewer, a candidate and a coach.

The thesis: cut along domain seams, put a versioned contract on every seam, give shared things one owner, integrate end to end by week four, and keep the contracts, the dependency graph and the milestones for yourself.
