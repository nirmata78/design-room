Pushing configuration to devices sounds like a message queue. But the devices are offline when you need them, untrusted, a truck roll away, and alive for seven years. A compact reference solution at the Staff bar (L6/L7): theory, a three-version design, an alternate path, and six challenges. 62 minutes, 481 numbered slides.

The thesis: never send a device a command; publish a desired state it converges to whenever it can. Release in waves that halt themselves, and give every device a last known good it returns to on its own.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Estimates: $1.5 M per 1 % bricked, 40,000 TLS handshakes a second in an unjittered storm, 50 TB per release
• Theory: desired vs reported state, edge vs level triggering, identity per device, waves by cohort, A/B slots with a watchdog, full jitter
• Six challenges: a config that crashes on boot, the rare hardware revision, six weeks offline, the reconnect storm, telemetry twice, and connected is not converged
