Merchants and a partner bank subscribe to a lender's loan and payment events. The first version is one POST inside the booking request, and it fails the first time a merchant's server takes 30 seconds. A full reference solution at the Staff bar (L6/L7), with the manager layer: theory, a three-version design, seven challenges that carry it to version six, a buy-against-build alternative, and the team seams. 89 minutes, 549 numbered slides, with an interviewer, a candidate and a coach.

The thesis: we promise at-least-once delivery and give every endpoint its own failure budget. Exactly-once is the consumer's job, and the contract that makes it possible is the product.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Estimates from one scale line: 600 calls in flight at the peak, 1,200 for one slow endpoint, 240,000 deliveries after a weekend outage
• Theory: six mechanisms (outbox, log, a queue per endpoint with a cap, full jitter with a breaker and a ramp, the consumer contract, an egress proxy) and an options matrix
• Seven challenges: publishing after the commit, one slow endpoint, a two-day outage, duplicates and order, SSRF and forged calls, a producer changing the payload, and "you never sent it"
• Components and technologies one level down, buy against build, and the contracts between teams (AUX)
