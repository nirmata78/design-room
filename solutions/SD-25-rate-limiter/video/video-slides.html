Rate limiter · syncstage 4
Why not a Redis increment per request?

It works at small scale. At a million a second:

Latency

About half a millisecond on every call: half the budget.

Hot shards

Heavy customers concentrate on single shards.

Coupled outage

A Redis incident becomes an API outage.

AlgorithmThe catch

Rate limiter · algorithmsstage 6
Key line · the production bug
Check-and-decrement must be atomic.
Read, compare, then write races under concurrency and lets traffic through. A Lua script, an atomic counter, or a single-threaded local bucket.
