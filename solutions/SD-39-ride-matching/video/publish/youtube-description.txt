Nearby drivers sounds like a geometry question. The geometry is the easy half: the hard half is that two riders can be handed the same driver in the same second. A compact reference solution at the Staff bar (L6/L7): theory, a three-version design, an alternate path, and six challenges. 61 minutes, 469 numbered slides.

The thesis: keep where drivers are as fast, overwrite-only, lossy memory, and keep who gets which driver as one exact conditional write, owned by one matcher per city.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Estimates: 1.25 million location writes a second, about 1 GB in memory, 2,100 claims a second
• Theory: H3 cells and k-rings, geohash vs S2 vs H3, soft state, fenced conditional claims, leases, batched assignment
• Six challenges: two riders one driver, the driver who vanishes, boundaries and hot cells, a lost shard, nearest is not closest, and "no drivers" next to idle drivers
