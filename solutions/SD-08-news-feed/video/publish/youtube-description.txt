Push or pull? Every book says "both". This video is about what comes after that word: the arithmetic that puts the line near ten thousand followers, what happens to an account that crosses it, how a deleted post leaves millions of copies, and what the feed does when the ranker is slow or a cache shard is gone. A full reference solution at the Staff bar (L6/L7): theory, a three-version design, eight challenges that break it, and the build cost. 92 minutes, 586 numbered slides.

The thesis: a feed is a materialised view that is cheap to read for almost everyone and impossible to precompute for a few. Push post IDs to the followers of ordinary accounts, pull the celebrities at read time, say where the line is and why, and keep every correctness rule on the read path.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Estimates as arithmetic: 90,000 opens a second, 18 million reads a second for pure pull, 15 minutes of fan-out for one celebrity post, 4 TB of IDs against 250 TB of bodies
• Theory: safety and liveness, seven mechanisms, an options matrix with a pick
• Eight challenges: the celebrity post, where the line is, delete and block, twice or never, a lost shard, a slow ranker, catch-up on 48 hours, and how you would know it got worse
• The build cost and the pull-to-push migration (marked AUX, the manager layer)

HOW TO USE IT
Run the round cold first. Then watch once straight through. On the second pass, pause after each interviewer question and answer it first. Slides badged AUX are the manager layer: skip them in a Staff round.
