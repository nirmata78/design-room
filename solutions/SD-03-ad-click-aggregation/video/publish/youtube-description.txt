Counting clicks sounds like n = n + 1. At thirty million events a second, with retries, phones that were offline for a day and a fraud team that changes its mind a week later, the hard part is counting once, in the right hour, and proving it. A Staff-bar (L6/L7) reference solution run like a real round: the design starts as a C4 container view and evolves only because an interviewer breaks it, six times, with each mechanism and technology introduced at the moment it is needed. 55 minutes, 0 numbered slides.

The thesis: count once, from one log, with one piece of code run twice, fast and provisional for the dashboard, exact and pinned for the bill. Never increment a number you can recompute: replace it, and keep every version you billed.

IN THIS VIDEO
• Estimates from one scale line: 10 M events a second, 6 GB/s at peak, 0.7 TB of dedup state, and $375 M a month in duplicate clicks if retries are not removed
• V1 → V2 → V3 on one C4 container view, with component zooms for every fix
• Six challenges: a dashboard that must be 10 s fresh, the same click three times (and a four-hour replay), late and skewed events, one ad at 2 million a second, fraud after the invoice, and a wrong number nobody notices
• Mechanisms where they are needed: one aggregation run twice, event IDs, watermarks, two-stage aggregation, replace-not-increment, versions and adjustments
